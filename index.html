Ceramic Coating

Long-lasting gloss. Easier maintenance.

Ceramic coating chemically bonds with your paint to create a durable, hydrophobic layer. It enhances gloss, helps resist UV exposure and environmental contamination, and makes routine washing easier. Unlike wax, which offers shorter-term protection, a professionally applied ceramic coating is built for lasting performance.

Starting at$599
Request Ceramic Coating Consultation

Ceramic coatings require a consultation and cannot be booked automatically.

Why ceramic?

  • Hydrophobic water behavior
  • UV and environmental protection
  • Enhanced depth and gloss
  • Simpler maintenance washes
  • Longer-lasting than traditional wax
