What is ceramic coating?

A durable layer for easier vehicle care.

Ceramic coating bonds to properly prepared paint, helping water bead away while improving gloss and resistance to UV exposure and environmental contamination. It does not make paint damage-proof, but it provides more lasting performance than traditional wax.

Benefits

  • Hydrophobic protection
  • Easier maintenance
  • Enhanced gloss
  • UV and chemical resistance
  • Long-term paint protection

Ceramic vs. Wax

FeatureCeramic coatingWax
LongevityLonger-term, product and care dependentShort-term
