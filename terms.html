Appointments and Estimates

Website prices are starting estimates. Final pricing may change based on vehicle size, condition, travel distance, selected add-ons, and work required. A request is not confirmed until accepted by Apex and the required deposit is received.

Deposits and Payment

A 20% deposit is expected when booking becomes active. The remaining balance is due after service. Secure payment processing is not enabled in the current front-end demo.

Cancellation and Weather

Customers may reschedule up to 24 hours before an appointment. Appointments canceled less than 24 hours before service may result in deposit forfeiture. Weather-related cancellations may be rescheduled without penalty when Apex determines conditions prevent safe service.

Vehicle Condition and Results

Customers should disclose stains, damage, pet hair, odors, loose items, and other conditions that may affect service. Significant improvement is the goal, but complete removal of every stain, odor, scratch, or defect cannot be guaranteed.

Access and Safety

Customers must provide authorized access to the vehicle and a safe, lawful service location with sufficient working space. Workplace or apartment service is subject to property rules.
